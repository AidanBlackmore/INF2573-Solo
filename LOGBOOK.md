**Author:** Aidan Blackmore

**Team members:** _TBD_

> A running individual record kept alongside the team project. Each entry ties the week's reading to a decision the team actually made, gives an honest account of my own contribution, reasons about the macro/systemic implications of what we're building, and discloses any AI use.

---

## Entry checklist (rubric reminder)

Each entry should cover:

- [ ] **Reading → project connection (30%)** — tie the reading to a *real* team decision, with insight (not a loose or generic link).
- [ ] **Individual contribution (20%)** — honest, specific account of my own role and growth (not "I helped").
- [ ] **Macro/systemic reflection (20%)** — socio-technical and macro-economic implications of the project.
- [ ] **Reflective honesty (20%)** — candid about uncertainty, mistakes, disagreement, and changes of mind.
- [ ] **Consistency & completeness (10%)** — substantive and on time.
- [ ] **AI use disclosure** — what tool, for what, and how I checked/changed its output.
